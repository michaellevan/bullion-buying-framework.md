# 🧠 Silver & Gold Buying Framework (Michael LeVan)

## 🎯 Core Philosophy
> Buy liquid, recognizable metal at the best possible price per ounce.
> Avoid getting trapped in premium, novelty, or hard-to-resell items.

---

# 🥇 Priority Targets (Auto-Buy Zone)

## 🦬 1 oz Buffalo Rounds
- Preferred generic silver
- High liquidity and recognizability
- Buy when:
  - ≤ $75 (strong)
  - ≤ $73–74 (excellent)

---

## 🍁 Sovereign Coins (Maple / Britannia / Philharmonic)
- Government-issued = strong resale
- Buy when:
  - ≤ $76 (good)
  - ≤ $75 (strong)
  - Near spot = excellent

---

## 🦬 Canadian 1.25 oz Bison
- Sovereign + unique size
- Evaluate **per ounce price**

### Buy thresholds:
- ≤ $75/oz = strong buy
- $75–77/oz = acceptable
- > $78/oz = pass

---

## 🦅 American Silver Eagles (ASE)
- Only buy near spot
- Imperfect ASEs are acceptable

### Buy thresholds:
- ≤ $75 = strong
- $76–78 = acceptable
- > $78 = pass

---

# 🥈 Secondary Targets (Situational)

## 🏭 Recognizable Bars (Sunshine, Asahi, etc.)
- Buy when:
  - Near spot (≤ $75)
- Avoid higher premiums

---

## 🪙 Fractional Gold
- Acceptable if premium is recoverable

### Buy guidelines:
- Compare to **AGE at spot baseline**
- Prefer:
  - Britannia
  - AGE
  - Philharmonic

### Avoid:
- 1g gold (too high premium)
- novelty or collectible gold

---

# ❌ Avoid (Default No-Buy List)

## 🚫 Premium / Collector Silver
- Proof coins
- Reverse proofs
- Slabbed coins (MS70, PF70)
- Limited mintage hype

---

## 🚫 Novelty / Licensed
- Star Wars, Marvel, Disney, etc.
- Colorized coins
- Gilded coins

---

## 🚫 Fractional Silver
- 1g, 5g, 10g bars
- 0.25 oz, 0.5 oz coins

> High premium + poor resale

---

## 🚫 40% Silver (Kennedy halves, etc.)
- Often trades below spot
- Weak demand and liquidity

---

## 🚫 Junk / Constitutional Silver (90%)
- Even if under spot:
  - Lower liquidity than bullion
  - Harder resale
- Only consider at **deep discount**

---

## 🚫 Vintage / Premium Bars
- Engelhard, Geiger, APMEX shapes
- Only buy if near spot (rare)

---

## 🚫 Foreign / Historical Coins
- Spanish reales, world silver, etc.
- Collector market required
- Not stackable bullion

---

# ⚠️ Deal Structure Rules

## ❌ Bundle Trap Rule
> If you must buy overpriced items to unlock a deal → PASS

---

## ❌ Mixed Lot Rule
> Mixed small lots usually hide high premiums → PASS

---

## ❌ "Cheap but Bad" Rule
> Cheap silver that is hard to sell is NOT a good deal

---

# 💰 Decision Framework

## Step 1: Convert to $/oz
- Always normalize price per ounce
- For 90%:
  - $1 FV = 0.715 oz

---

## Step 2: Compare to baseline

| Type | Target Price |
|------|------------|
| Best deals (Buffalo) | ~$73–75 |
| Sovereign coins | ~$74–76 |
| Acceptable max | ~$77 |

---

## Step 3: Liquidity Check

> “Would someone WANT this tomorrow?”

- Yes → consider
- No → pass

---

# 🧠 Final Rule

> Do NOT buy something you don’t want just because it’s “a deal”

---

# 🏁 Strategy Summary

## Focus on:
- Buffalos
- Maples / Britannias
- Occasional ASE near spot
- Select fractional gold

## Avoid:
- Premium distractions
- Collector traps
- Low-liquidity silver
