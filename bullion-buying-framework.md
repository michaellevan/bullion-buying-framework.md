# Bullion Buying Framework

## Market Baselines
- Gold: 1 oz American Gold Eagles available at spot (JM Bullion baseline)
- Silver: Junk/constitutional silver available under spot (not preferred)
- Preferred silver: ASEs, Maples, Britannias near spot

---

## Personal Preferences
- Priority: 5 oz America the Beautiful (ATB) coins
- Do NOT buy ATBs already owned
- Prefer Buffalo rounds for generic silver
- Prefer recognizable sovereign bullion over obscure rounds
- Fractional gold acceptable if premium is recoverable on resale

## Buying Hierarchy

### Tier 1 (Instant Buy)
- ATB coins at or near melt (not already owned)
- ASEs at or near spot
- Maples/Britannias at or near spot
- Buffalo rounds at or under spot
- Recognizable silver below spot
- 1 oz gold (AGE) at spot

---

### Tier 2 (Good Buy)
- Fractional gold with low, recoverable premium
- Sovereign silver with small premium
- Fairly priced ATB quarter sets

---

### Tier 3 (Usually Pass)
- Generic silver ~ $77/oz (unless beating better alternatives)
- High premium collectible silver
- Fractional silver
- Obscure rounds
- Slabbed modern silver with high premium
- Gold with premium worse than AGE at spot

---

## Decision Filter (10 Seconds)

1. Is it:
   - ATB near melt (not owned)?
   - ASE/Maple/Brit near spot?
   - Buffalo near/under spot?
   - Gold near spot or recoverable premium?

→ If YES: BUY

2. If NO:
→ Would I rather buy:
   - ATB near melt
   - ASE at spot
   - Gold at spot?

→ If YES: SKIP

---

## Scoring Model

### Silver
- 10/10: ATB near melt, sovereign at spot, or under spot
- 8/10: ASE/Maple/Brit small premium
- 6/10: acceptable sovereign premium
- 4/10: generics not beating alternatives
- 2/10: collectible/novelty premium

### Gold
- 10/10: 1 oz AGE at spot
- 8/10: fractional at recoverable premium
- 6/10: pre-33 with reasonable premium
- 3/10: high premium small gold

---

## Key Rule

Always compare against:
- ATB near melt
- ASE/Maple/Brit near spot
- Gold at spot

If it doesn’t beat those → pass
